## Evidence-backed findings

The index proves that this manual covers the main TOP II AD machine, electrical and safety controls, the SMC air-treatment subsystem, CR27 synchronization, electronic-cam control, product-format changeovers, cleaning/maintenance plans, lubrication and line-speed optimization. The index names the cam display `KET39`, while photographed page 69 names it `CR30`; both identifiers must remain linked until the physical hardware resolves the discrepancy.

It does not yet prove the exact values, procedures, intervals, wiring, settings, tolerances, or installed options. Those must be extracted from the referenced pages and verified against the physical machine.

4. Pages 119–130 for lubrication.
