CURRENT INTERPRETATION
• Communication: Card 01 is online and returning detailed live data to the CR30.
• Temperature: 36 C is far below the manual's approximate 85 C high-temperature threshold.
• Low-voltage supply: 25.0 V is consistent with a nominal 24 V control supply.
• Motor-channel balance: IA and IB are both 2.8 A. LA is 7.4 mH and LB is 7.0 mH; the two channels
are close, with no obvious missing phase in this snapshot.
• Program activity: Pc was captured at 374. A second reading while the machine operates is required
to prove that the instruction pointer advances.
• Sensor/input behavior: Nc=1.45 and Sx=01 are snapshots. Their diagnostic meaning depends on
whether they change with the associated photocell and input states.
• Address versus physical head: CARD 01 is confirmed as network address 01. It must not be
assumed to be physical platform/head 1 until the DIP-switch address or physical mapping is verified.
COMPARISON REQUIRED FOR THE SUSPECTED CARD
Capture the same complete sequence under the same machine condition. Compare Ve, LV, LA/LB,
IA/IB, Tp, Pc, Nc, Sx and En. A firmware mismatch, abnormal supply, missing motor channel, high
temperature, frozen program counter, or missing sensor response will materially narrow the fault.
