1. REVIEW METHOD
Use the HMI snapshot as a point-in-time operating record. Keep the tag identity separate from its temporary reading. Example: PMP104 is the device; 49 Hz is the snapshot value. A
relationship such as PMP104 CIRCULATES heating water is separately confidence-marked.
MARK MEANING HOW TO USE IT
CONFIRMED Directly visible on HMI/nameplate or otherwise
established
May be used as a working fact; still verify before hazardous work.
LIKELY Strongly supported by HMI placement, standard
instrumentation usage, or physical context
Use for mapping; field-check piping/tubing/wiring.
BEST GUESS Plausible expansion/function but not established Keep in graph so nothing is lost; actively verify.
UNKNOWN Not enough evidence Do not expand or invent. Record exact tag and investigate.
Important correction rule: Never invent PLC I/O addresses, ladder rungs, interlocks, valve fail action, or exact pipe routing from appearance alone. Any earlier conceptual ladder drawing containing
addresses should be treated as illustrative only, not machine logic.

7. ASSOCIATED LITERATURE - FAIRCHILD TT6000-401 REVIEW NOTES
This section condenses the manufacturer-family information most useful at the cabinet. It is a review aid, not a substitute for the manufacturer's complete IOM.
ITEM REFERENCE NOTE FOR MANUAL REVIEW
Manufacturer / family Fairchild Industrial Products - Model T6000 family; observed unit marked TT6000-401.
Device function Electro-pneumatic transducer: converts a DC electrical input signal into a proportional pneumatic output.
Observed -401 configuration 4-20 mA input; 3-15 PSIG output; observed nameplate supply range 20-120 PSIG.
TT prefix TT6000 is the terminal-block version in the T6000 family.
Mounting T6000 literature describes DIN-rail mounting using the 16893 mounting kit; this matches the cabinet installation style being reviewed.
Pneumatic ports T6000 documentation shows IN and OUT ports and a vent. Unused ports are plugged. The vent/exhaust must not be treated as a
process-air connection.
Why air can be heard at the
body/back
The T6000 is a pneumatic relay/nozzle-flapper device and has a vent/exhaust path. A small operational bleed/exhaust can be normal; a large
continuous leak at steady command should be compared against the IOM and checked for contamination, damaged internals, supply issues,
or downstream behavior.
Calibration ZERO and SPAN adjustments exist. Do not adjust until input current, supply pressure, output pressure, direction of action, and downstream
actuator behavior are verified.
Direction T6000 family literature supports forward/direct and reverse-acting adjustment. Therefore 15 PSIG cannot automatically be called 'valve open'
until the actuator/valve action is verified.
Safety Steam, hot product, instrument air and energized control circuits can injure personnel. Use plant LOTO/process procedures and rated test
equipment; do not defeat interlocks for troubleshooting.
Manufacturer literature to keep with this packet: Fairchild T6000 Electro-Pneumatic Transducer Installation, Operation & Maintenance Instructions. Search/print the current T6000 IOM from
Fairchild/Rotork or an authorized distributor and verify that it covers the exact TT6000-401 revision/date code installed.
