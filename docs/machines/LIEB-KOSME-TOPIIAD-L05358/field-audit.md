# Kosme TOP II AD CR30 field audit

Field procedure for preserving card data, comparing firmware and temperature, confirming unique DIP addresses, separating one-board from all-board communication failures, and recording a controlled replacement/copy result.

[Open or download the complete field-audit PDF](assets/evidence/Industrial%20Asset%20Graph%20-%20Kosme%20Sensicol%20Labeler%20Reliability/03%20Live%20Troubleshooting/Kosme_TOP_II_AD_CR30_Field_Audit_Cheat_Sheet.pdf)

Qualified personnel must apply plant LOTO and follow the OEM safety warnings before removing protections or touching the card assemblies.
